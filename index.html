Tap Open to choose a PDF. It stays on this phone; nothing is uploaded.

Press Play, or tap any line in the PDF to start reading from there. A red underline follows the sentence being read.

For natural Indian voices, tap ⚙ and download the Hindi (Pratham) and English (Navgurukul) voice packs. Until then the phone's own voices are used. To use the app without internet, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

Scanned copies (Hindi or English) can be read too: the app offers a one-time 8 MB page-reading download when it needs it.
