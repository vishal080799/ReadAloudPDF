Tap Open to choose a PDF. It stays on this phone; nothing is uploaded.

Press Play, or tap any line in the PDF to start reading from there. A red underline follows the sentence being read.

Speech uses this phone's own Hindi and English (India) voices. To use the app without internet, open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

Works with text-based PDFs. Scanned PDFs need OCR first.
